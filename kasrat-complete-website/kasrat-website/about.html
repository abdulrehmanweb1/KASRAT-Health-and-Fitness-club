Fazeel Qureshi is the CEO of Kasrat Fitness Club, where he leads efforts to promote health and wellness through innovative fitness solutions. With a strong background in business management, he has successfully grown the club into a leading fitness destination. Additionally, he is a seasoned expert in the real estate field, leveraging his knowledge to navigate the complexities of property investment and development. His entrepreneurial spirit and commitment to excellence drive both his ventures in fitness and real estate, making him a prominent figure in both industries.
